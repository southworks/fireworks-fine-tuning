The derived dataset was created from the `Industrial_and_Scientific` category of Amazon Reviews 2023 by McAuley Lab.

- Project: https://amazon-reviews-2023.github.io/
- Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/McAuley-Lab/Amazon-Reviews-2023

## Banking77

`data/banking77/` is derived from Banking77 by PolyAI, released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
license (CC BY 4.0).

- Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/PolyAI/banking77
- Paper: Casanueva et al., "Efficient Intent Detection with Dual Sentence Encoders" (2020), https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04807

The files were converted to chat format and the original training split was divided into training and
validation (stratified 10%, seed 42). No other changes were made.
